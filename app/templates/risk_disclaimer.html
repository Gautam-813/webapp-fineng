No financial advice or profit guarantee.

TheFinanceCompany provides software tools and related technical services. We do not provide personalized investment, financial, legal, tax, or brokerage advice.

1. Trading involves substantial risk

Foreign exchange, commodities, contracts for difference, indices, digital assets, and other financial markets can be volatile. You may lose some or all of the money you trade. In leveraged or margin trading, losses can occur quickly and may exceed your initial deposit depending on your broker, account type, and local rules.

You should trade only with funds you can afford to lose and should carefully consider your experience, objectives, financial situation, and risk tolerance before using any trading software.

2. Software does not guarantee performance

Expert Advisors, indicators, trade copiers, dashboards, scripts, and automation tools are software. They may help follow rules, monitor markets, or execute instructions, but they cannot predict markets or guarantee profits.

Past performance, backtests, screenshots, demo results, optimization reports, or examples are not guarantees of future results. Market conditions can change, execution quality can vary, and technical issues can affect outcomes.

3. You remain responsible for trading decisions

You are responsible for selecting products, configuring settings, choosing brokers, deciding position sizes, monitoring live activity, and deciding whether software is suitable for your use. You should test any tool in a demo environment before using it with live funds.

4. Technical and operational risks

Automated trading can be affected by internet outages, VPS failures, broker execution delays, spreads, slippage, platform updates, incorrect settings, account restrictions, market gaps, and third-party service interruptions.

You should monitor active systems and use risk limits, maximum drawdown controls, and emergency stop procedures where appropriate.

5. Regulatory and broker responsibility

You are responsible for ensuring that your trading activity, broker relationship, software use, and account setup comply with applicable laws, regulations, broker terms, and exchange or market rules in your jurisdiction.

6. Contact before purchase

If you are unsure whether a product is suitable for your intended use, contact us before placing an order. We can explain software features and technical behavior, but we cannot tell you whether a trade or strategy is financially suitable for you.
