Privacy Policy

This draft explains what information the site may collect, why it is used, and how users can contact us about privacy requests.

This page should be updated whenever analytics, payments, email tools, hosting, licensing, or support tools are added.

1. Information we collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including name, email address, phone number, company name, account credentials, order information, product inquiries, support messages, custom project requirements, and related communication details.

We may also collect technical information such as browser type, IP address, device information, pages visited, timestamps, and security logs when needed for site operation, security, analytics, or troubleshooting.

2. How we use information

We use information to operate the website, manage accounts, process orders, respond to inquiries, review custom project requests, provide support, improve products, prevent abuse, maintain security, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Payments and licensing

The current demo checkout does not collect payment. When payment processing and licensing are added, relevant order, payment, license, and delivery information may be processed by payment providers, licensing systems, email services, hosting providers, and support tools.

4. Cookies and sessions

The website may use cookies or similar technologies for login sessions, cart behavior, security, preferences, and analytics. You can control cookies through your browser settings, but some features may not work correctly without them.

5. Sharing information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that help operate the website, process payments, deliver emails, host infrastructure, provide support, or maintain security. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and system integrity.

6. Data security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information. No system is completely secure, so users should also protect their login credentials and notify us about suspected unauthorized access.

7. Data retention

We keep information for as long as needed to operate the service, support users, maintain business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations. Retention periods may vary by data type.

8. Your choices

You may contact us to request access, correction, deletion, or other privacy-related assistance. Some requests may be limited by account security, order records, legal obligations, or fraud-prevention requirements.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact page and include "Privacy Request" in the subject line.
